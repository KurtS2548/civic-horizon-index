Transparent Public Data

Designed for Understanding

Civic Horizon Index presents aggregated participant data clearly while acknowledging the limits of voluntary public surveys.

Aggregated Results

Public scores are calculated from combined survey responses rather than displaying individual submissions.

Consistent Measurement

Every participant rates the same ten issues using the same one-to-ten priority scale.

Participation Matters

Results reflect voluntary participants and should not automatically be treated as a scientific sample of the entire population.
