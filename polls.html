Why Participate?

Every Response Adds to the Larger Picture

One response cannot define a nation. When many people share their priorities, however, the combined results can reveal meaningful patterns among those who choose to participate.

Civic Horizon Index is designed to help people learn, participate, and explore—not to tell anyone what they should believe.
