Our Mission

Helping America understand itself through the collective voices of its people.

Civic Horizon Index provides a transparent, nonpartisan place where people can learn how government works, share what matters most to them, and explore the priorities expressed by participants across states and communities.

Learn More About Our Mission →

Independent

The platform is designed to present educational information and participant data without political party alignment or editorial influence.

Transparent

Scores, response counts, learning materials, and methodology are presented clearly.

Privacy Conscious

Public results are displayed in aggregate rather than revealing individual survey responses.

Built for Understanding

The goal is not to tell people what to think, but to help them learn, participate, and explore.
