Quick Guide

How the Branches Check One Another

Legislative

Passes laws, controls funding, conducts oversight, confirms many appointments, and may override vetoes.

Executive

Carries out laws, vetoes bills, nominates judges and officials, and directs the executive branch.

Judicial

Resolves legal disputes and may review whether laws and government actions comply with the Constitution.
