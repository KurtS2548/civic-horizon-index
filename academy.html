Why Civic Knowledge Matters

Understanding the System Strengthens Your Voice

Knowing which office, branch, or level of government holds responsibility helps people direct questions, concerns, and participation to the right place.

Civic Academy does not tell visitors which policies or political parties to support. It explains how the system operates so people can participate with greater confidence and understanding.

Questions Civic Academy Will Answer

  • Who passes federal laws?
  • Can the president create laws alone?
  • Who controls local public schools?
  • Which government issues driver's licenses?
  • How can Congress limit presidential power?
  • How can citizens influence government between elections?
