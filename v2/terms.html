Terms of Use

Clear rules for using Civic Horizon

These Terms of Use describe the basic rules for accessing Civic Horizon Index, participating in civic features, maintaining an account, and using information provided through the platform.

Core Principle Use Civic Horizon honestly, respectfully, and for legitimate civic participation. These terms are intended to protect participants, platform integrity, and the reliability of Civic Horizon results.

Agreement

Using Civic Horizon means agreeing to these terms

By creating an account, signing in, participating, or otherwise using Civic Horizon Index, you agree to follow these Terms of Use and applicable laws.

Important

If you do not agree with these terms, you should not use Civic Horizon participation features. These terms may be updated as the platform changes.

Accounts

Participants are responsible for their accounts

Civic Horizon uses participant accounts to help protect access, voting eligibility, and result integrity.

Accurate Information

Provide truthful account information

Participants should provide accurate information when creating or maintaining an account, particularly when information is used to determine participation eligibility.

Account Security

Protect your login credentials

Participants are responsible for taking reasonable steps to protect access to their account and should not knowingly allow others to use the account to submit responses.

One Participant

Do not create accounts to manipulate results

Creating duplicate, false, automated, or misleading accounts for the purpose of influencing Civic Horizon results is prohibited.

Minimum Age

Participants must be at least 13 years old

Civic Horizon accounts and participation features are currently available only to individuals age 13 and older. Individuals under age 13 may not create or use a Civic Horizon participant account.

Participation

Polls and surveys must be used honestly

Civic Horizon participation features are designed to measure genuine participant views.

01

Submit only your own response

Participants should answer polls, surveys, and approval trackers based on their own views rather than submitting responses on behalf of another person.

02

Respect participation limits

Participants may not attempt to bypass one-response rules, voting periods, geographic eligibility, or other controls designed to protect participation integrity.

03

No automated voting

Bots, scripts, automated tools, or other systems may not be used to submit votes, survey responses, or other participation activity unless Civic Horizon expressly authorizes that use.

04

Do not interfere with platform integrity

Attempts to alter results, access protected information, disrupt services, exploit security weaknesses, or interfere with other participants are prohibited.

Geographic Eligibility

Some participation is limited by jurisdiction

State, district, and other local civic features may require geographic eligibility.

Location

Geographic information must be accurate

Participants should not intentionally provide false location information to gain access to voting opportunities outside their eligible jurisdiction.

Eligibility

Civic Horizon determines participation access

The platform may use available geographic information to determine whether an account is eligible to participate in a specific state, district, or local civic feature.

Viewing

Reading information may remain unrestricted

Civic information and results may remain viewable even when a participant is not eligible to submit a response in that jurisdiction.

Civic Poll Content

Poll questions must be appropriate for the platform

If Civic Horizon allows participants to suggest, submit, or create civic questions, those submissions must follow platform standards.

01

Questions should have a legitimate civic purpose

Poll submissions should relate to civic, public-policy, government, community, or other appropriate public-interest topics.

02

No harassment or targeted abuse

Civic Horizon may reject or remove content that is primarily intended to threaten, harass, intimidate, humiliate, or target private individuals.

03

No deceptive or manipulative submissions

Questions designed primarily to mislead, impersonate others, manipulate results, or falsely represent Civic Horizon may be rejected or removed.

04

Civic Horizon may moderate submissions

Poll suggestions or participant-created content may be reviewed, edited for clarity, declined, deactivated, or removed when necessary to protect neutrality, safety, legality, or platform quality.

Results and Information

Civic Horizon information has important limitations

Results and civic information should be interpreted according to the Methodology and Neutrality Statement.

Participation Results

Results reflect participating users

Civic Horizon results generally reflect voluntary participants and are not automatically representative of the broader population.

Civic Information

Information may change

Public officials, government records, statistics, laws, district boundaries, and other civic information may change over time.

Verification

Errors may occur

Civic Horizon works to provide accurate information, but the platform does not guarantee that every piece of information will always be complete, current, or error-free.

Independent civic platform

Civic Horizon Index is an independent civic participation platform. It is not a government agency, election authority, or official voting system. Votes, ratings, polls, surveys, and other responses submitted through Civic Horizon are not official ballots or votes in a government election.

Information Only

Civic Horizon is an informational civic platform

Not professional advice

Civic Horizon content is provided for civic, educational, and informational purposes. It should not be treated as legal, financial, tax, medical, or other professional advice.

Security abuse

Civic Horizon may restrict accounts that repeatedly or seriously violate these Terms of Use.
