Privacy

Privacy should be clear, not hidden

Civic Horizon Index uses participant information to operate accounts, protect voting integrity, determine eligibility, and provide civic features. This page explains the types of information used and how they are handled.

Core Principle Collect only what serves a legitimate purpose. Civic Horizon is designed to separate private account information from public participation results whenever practical.

Information We Use

Information needed to operate Civic Horizon

Different parts of the platform require different types of information.

Account Information

Information used to manage your account

Civic Horizon may use account information such as an email address, authentication status, profile information, and other information needed to maintain access to the platform.

Eligibility Information

Information used to determine where you can participate

Geographic information, such as ZIP code and resolved jurisdiction data, may be used to determine eligibility for state, district, or other location-specific participation.

Participation Information

Poll and survey responses

Civic Horizon stores responses submitted to polls, surveys, approval trackers, national priorities, and other civic participation features.

How Information Is Used

Participant information should serve a clear purpose

Civic Horizon uses information to operate the platform, protect participation, and produce civic results.

01

Account access and authentication

Account information is used to sign participants in, maintain account access, and support account-related features.

02

Participation eligibility

Civic Horizon may use account and geographic information to determine whether a participant is eligible to vote in a specific poll, approval tracker, or jurisdiction.

03

Duplicate-vote prevention

Private participation records may be used to determine whether an account has already participated in a poll or recurring voting period.

04

Civic results and analysis

Valid responses may be combined with other participant responses to calculate percentages, rankings, averages, and other Civic Horizon results.

Public and Private Data

Account identity and public results should remain separate

Civic Horizon is designed so public civic results do not need to expose a participant's private account identity.

Private Records

Account records remain private

Information used for authentication, participation eligibility, account history, and duplicate-vote prevention is not intended to be displayed publicly as part of poll results.

Public Results

Results are shown in aggregate

Civic Horizon generally displays totals, percentages, averages, rankings, and similar aggregate results rather than identifying individual participants.

Geographic Results

Location may be used for grouped results

Geographic information may be used to produce state, district, or other jurisdiction-level participation results without publicly displaying a participant's individual account identity.

Data Minimization

Avoid collecting information that is not needed

Civic Horizon should not collect personal information simply because it may be useful someday.

01

Collect for a defined purpose

Personal information should be collected only when it supports a specific account, eligibility, security, participation, or platform function.

02

Avoid unnecessary exposure

Private participant information should not be displayed publicly when aggregate or anonymous information is sufficient.

03

Review data practices as the platform grows

New Civic Horizon features should be reviewed to determine whether additional information is genuinely necessary before it is collected.

Security

Protecting participant information

Civic Horizon uses technical controls intended to reduce unauthorized access, duplicate voting, and improper changes to participation data.

Authentication

Account access controls

Authentication is used to help ensure that protected account and participation features are associated with an authorized participant.

Database Controls

Rules help restrict access

Database access rules and application logic are used to help restrict unauthorized writes and protect private participation records.

Limitations

No system is completely risk-free

Civic Horizon works to protect participant information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Service Providers

Technology providers may help operate the platform

Civic Horizon may rely on third-party technology services for hosting, authentication, databases, security, or other platform functions.

Important

Information handled by outside service providers may also be subject to their own privacy and security practices. Civic Horizon should use established providers and limit shared information to what is reasonably necessary for the service being provided.

Participant Choices

Questions about your information

Civic Horizon should provide reasonable ways for participants to ask questions about account or privacy information.

Account

Review account information

Participants may be able to review or update certain account information through their Civic Horizon profile.

Questions

Contact Civic Horizon

Participants can use the Civic Horizon contact page to ask questions about privacy or information associated with their account.

Future Controls

Privacy controls may expand

Additional account, deletion, export, or privacy controls may be added as Civic Horizon develops and legal requirements are reviewed.

Youth Participation

Younger participants require additional care

Civic Horizon may support age-based participation experiences. Privacy and eligibility practices for younger users should be designed with additional safeguards and reviewed against applicable legal requirements.

Before public launch

Youth participation, parental consent requirements, age thresholds, and data-handling practices should receive a dedicated legal and privacy review before the platform is opened broadly to minors.

Policy Information

Privacy practices may evolve as Civic Horizon grows

Civic Horizon may update this privacy statement as features, technology, legal requirements, and data practices change.
