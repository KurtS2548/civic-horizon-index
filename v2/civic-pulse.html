Understanding the Numbers

Transparent by design

Civic Pulse results represent responses from Civic Horizon Index participants. Unless a future methodology specifically uses representative sampling and weighting, these results should not be described as a scientific estimate of all Americans.

The goal is to provide a transparent view of participant sentiment and how it changes over time.

Nonpartisan presentation

Civic Horizon Index does not endorse a political party, candidate, officeholder, institution, or policy position.
