Methodology

How to interpret the data

Civic Horizon Index is a participation platform, not a traditional probability-based polling firm. That distinction matters when reading the results.

01

Participation is voluntary

Results reflect people who choose to participate in Civic Horizon Index surveys and polls.

02

National Priorities use 1–10 ratings

Participants rate each issue from lower priority to highest priority. Average scores are then used to build the live rankings.

03

Community polls are question-specific

Each community poll measures responses only to that individual question and should be interpreted alongside its participation total.

04

Results update as participation grows

Rankings and percentages can change whenever new responses are submitted, so results represent a current snapshot rather than a fixed conclusion.

Important limitation

Unless a future survey uses a scientifically designed sampling method, Civic Horizon Index results should not be described as representative of all Americans.
