Our Mission

Helping Americans understand, participate in, and strengthen civic life.

Civic Horizon Index is an independent civic engagement platform designed to help people better understand the priorities of their communities and the nation. We believe informed participation, respectful dialogue, and transparent public opinion are essential to a healthy democracy.

Our goal is not to persuade people what to think. Our goal is to provide trustworthy tools that help citizens explore issues, participate in surveys, learn how government works, and better understand the perspectives of others.

Neutral

Every feature is designed to present information fairly without promoting a political viewpoint.

Transparent

Results are calculated openly so participants can understand how public opinion is represented.

Educational

We believe better civic understanding leads to stronger participation and better conversations.
