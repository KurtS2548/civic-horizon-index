Our Mission

Better civic understanding starts with better information.

Civic Horizon Index is an independent, nonpartisan civic engagement platform designed to help people understand public priorities, participate in civic questions, and learn how government works.

Our goal is not to tell people what to think. It is to provide transparent tools that make civic participation easier to understand and easier to use.

Neutral

Information is presented without political endorsement or persuasion.

Transparent

Participants can see how results are collected and represented.

Accessible

Civic information should be clear, understandable, and easy to participate in.
