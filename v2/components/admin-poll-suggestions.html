Before approving a suggestion

01 Public relevance

Does the issue meaningfully affect the public or the selected community?

02 Neutral wording

Can the question be asked without favoring one answer, party, candidate, ideology, or desired result?

03 Clear language

Will participants understand what they are being asked without unnecessary jargon?

04 No duplication

Is the topic meaningfully different from a poll that is already active or recently completed?

05 Respect for everyone

Does the suggestion address an issue or idea rather than attacking a person or group?

06 Useful results

Will the answers provide meaningful information about what participants actually think?

REVIEW ASSISTANCE

Need help reviewing a large number of suggestions?

Suggestions can be reviewed in batches for neutrality, clarity, public relevance, duplication, partisan wording, and compliance with Civic Horizon standards.
