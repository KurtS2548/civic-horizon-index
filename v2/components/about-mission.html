Our Mission

Make civic participation easier to understand and easier to use

Civic Horizon Index gives people a clear place to express their views, explore public priorities, understand civic information, and see how others are responding.

01

Listen

Give participants clear and neutral ways to share their priorities, opinions, and assessments of public issues and elected officials.

02

Inform

Turn participation into understandable results, rankings, comparisons, and civic information without political scoring or endorsement.

03

Educate

Help people better understand government, public institutions, and civic decision-making through clear educational tools.

Why Civic Horizon Index exists

People deserve a clearer way to participate in civic life.

Civic Horizon Index is built around a simple idea: people should have an accessible place to express what matters to them, see how others are responding, learn about the government that represents them, and explore civic information without needing a background in politics or data analysis.

The platform is designed as a participation, information, and learning tool. Civic Horizon Index does not claim that its participants automatically form a scientifically representative sample of the United States.
