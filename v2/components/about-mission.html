Our Mission

Make civic priorities easier to understand

Civic Horizon Index gives people a simple place to express what matters to them and see how those priorities compare with the broader community.

Inform

Why Civic Horizon Index exists

Public debate is louder when priorities are unclear.

Civic Horizon Index is built around a straightforward idea: people should have an accessible way to express their priorities, see how others are responding, and explore civic information without requiring a background in politics or data analysis.

The platform is designed as a participation and learning tool. It does not claim that its users automatically form a scientifically representative sample of the United States.
