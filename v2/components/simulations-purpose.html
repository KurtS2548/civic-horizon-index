Why Simulations?

Learn by making decisions

Reading can explain the formal responsibilities of government. Simulations help visitors understand the tradeoffs, relationships, limits, and consequences that shape public leadership.

These experiences are educational and nonpartisan. They are designed to demonstrate government processes rather than promote a political party, candidate, or policy position.

Skills You Will Practice

  • Listening to constituents
  • Building coalitions
  • Negotiating compromises
  • Understanding constitutional limits
  • Balancing competing priorities
  • Explaining decisions to the public
