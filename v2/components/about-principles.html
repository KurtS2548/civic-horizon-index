Guiding Principles

Built around trust, neutrality, and clarity

Civic Horizon Index is designed to make civic participation useful while being clear about where information comes from and what participant results actually represent.

Neutrality

Inform without telling people what to think

Questions and civic information should be presented as clearly and fairly as possible without promoting a political party, candidate, or viewpoint.

Transparency

Make results understandable

Participation totals, results, sources, and important limitations should be presented clearly so people can understand what the information represents.

Privacy

Collect only what serves a purpose

Civic Horizon should avoid unnecessary personal information and use participant data only when it serves a legitimate platform or civic purpose.

Context

Separate participation from fact

Participant results show what Civic Horizon participants think. Factual civic information should be identified separately and supported by reliable sources whenever appropriate.
