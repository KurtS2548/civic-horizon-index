Guiding Principles

Built around trust and clarity

Civic Horizon Index is designed to be useful without pretending the data says more than it really does.

Neutrality

Ask questions without steering answers

Poll wording should be as clear and balanced as possible so participants can respond based on their own views.

Transparency

Show how the platform works

Results, participation totals, and important limitations should be easy to understand rather than hidden behind technical language.

Privacy

Collect only what is useful

The platform should avoid unnecessary personal information and use participant data only for meaningful civic analysis.

Context

Be clear about what results mean

Civic Horizon Index participation data reflects people who choose to take part. It should not be presented as a scientific national sample unless the methodology supports that claim.
