TRANSPARENCY

How We Choose Poll Questions

We believe people should know how Civic Horizon decides which questions appear on the platform.

Civic Horizon Index does not automatically publish submitted questions. Suggestions are reviewed before they can become active polls.

We consider whether a proposed question:

  • Addresses an issue relevant to the public.
  • Can be written in clear and neutral language.
  • Does not push participants toward a particular answer.
  • Is based on a real policy, issue, proposal, or public concern.
  • Is not substantially duplicated by an existing poll.
  • Can produce meaningful and understandable results.
  • Is appropriate for the geographic level selected.
Popularity alone does not determine which questions are published.

A frequently suggested topic may receive additional consideration, but Civic Horizon will not select, reject, or word questions to favor a political party, candidate, ideology, or desired result.

We may rewrite a submitted question to improve neutrality, accuracy, or clarity. The final wording will be held to the same standards as every other Civic Horizon poll.

Submitting a question does not guarantee that it will be published.

Our goal is simple: ask worthwhile questions fairly, show the results accurately, and be transparent about how we got there.

OUR STANDARD

Civic Horizon Is for Everyone

Civic Horizon Index exists to give all Americans a fair opportunity to be heard.

People are welcome here regardless of political affiliation, race, ethnicity, sex, gender, background, beliefs, location, or economic circumstances.

No Hate. No Harassment. No Bullying.

Hate, racism, sexism, harassment, bullying, threats, or attacks against individuals or groups will not be tolerated.

Poll suggestions must address issues and ideas — not attack people or groups of people.

Nonpartisan by Design

Civic Horizon will not select, reject, or word poll questions to benefit a political party, candidate, ideology, or preferred outcome.

Politically difficult or controversial subjects can and should be discussed when they matter to Americans. Our responsibility is to ask about them neutrally and present the results fairly.

Suggestions that violate these standards will not be published.

Our purpose is not to divide people. It is to understand what people actually think.
