Our Standard

Questions and criticism are welcome

Civic Horizon Index is designed to improve through participant feedback. Questions, concerns, corrections, and constructive criticism are welcome regardless of political viewpoint.

Messages should remain focused on legitimate questions, problems, feedback, or civic concerns rather than harassment, threats, spam, or attempts to disrupt the platform.
