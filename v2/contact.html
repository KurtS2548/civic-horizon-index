OUR STANDARD

Civic Horizon Is for Everyone

Civic Horizon Index exists to give all Americans a fair opportunity to be heard.

Questions, feedback, concerns, and constructive criticism are welcome. Our goal is to make Civic Horizon accurate, understandable, transparent, and useful.

Respectful Participation

Hate, harassment, bullying, threats, or attacks against individuals or groups are not acceptable on Civic Horizon.

Nonpartisan by Design

Civic Horizon is designed to measure and understand public opinion, not to promote a political party, candidate, ideology, or preferred outcome.

Our purpose is not to divide people. It is to understand what people actually think.
