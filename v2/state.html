About state civic information

Civic Horizon Index will use public and official government sources whenever possible. Congressional voting records will reflect official roll-call data. Financial information will be clearly labeled as public disclosure information or estimated ranges when appropriate. Only public office phone numbers will be displayed; private contact information and street addresses will not be published.
